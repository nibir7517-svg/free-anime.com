Anime

An anime series Anime is a form of animated entertainment that originated in Japan.
It is popular amongchildren, teenagers, and adults all over the world. Anime includes
many different genres, such as action, adventure, fantasy, comedy, mystery, and science
fiction. Unlike ordinary cartoons, many anime series have deep stories, interesting
characters, and exciting adventures.
